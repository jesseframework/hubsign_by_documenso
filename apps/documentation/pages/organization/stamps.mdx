# Stamps

**Settings → Documents → Stamps** is a shared library of marks — a company seal, a
registration number, a "PAID" or "RECEIVED" stamp — that can be placed on a document
and burned into the finished PDF.

Stamps are **organization-scoped**: every member can use every stamp in the library,
so a seal is defined once rather than re-uploaded by each person who needs it.

## Three kinds

| Kind | Made by |
|---|---|
| **Uploaded** | You supply an image — a scan of a physical seal, a logo |
| **Designed** | Built in the editor from text and shapes |
| **AI generated** | Generated, then editable like a designed stamp |

## Dynamic placeholders

A stamp can carry tokens that are filled in at the moment it is applied, rather than
being fixed text:

```
{date}
{signer.name}
```

This is what makes a "RECEIVED" stamp useful — the date is the date it was actually
applied, not the date somebody typed into the design.

## When a stamp becomes part of the document

Stamps are drawn into the PDF during **sealing**, along with signature fields and the
audit certificate.

<Callout type="warning">
  That means a stamp is permanent once the document completes, and a stamp added to the
  library afterwards cannot appear on a document that is already sealed. Get the stamp
  right before sending, not after.
</Callout>

Stamp placement is best-effort: if a stamp cannot be drawn for some reason, the seal
still completes and the failure is logged rather than blocking the signature. A
document is more important than its ornament — but it does mean you should check that
a stamp you rely on actually appears on the finished PDF the first time you use it.

## A note on seals as legal marks

In some jurisdictions a company seal carries legal weight. HubSign draws the image
you give it; it does not verify that whoever applied it was entitled to.

If a seal in your organization is meaningful rather than decorative, control **who can
send documents** rather than relying on the stamp library being restricted — every
member can use every stamp.
