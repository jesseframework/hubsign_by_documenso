# Dashboard

**Home** reports live signing activity for your whole
organization, not just your own documents.

## What it shows

| Group | Figures |
|---|---|
| **Headline** | Documents sent, completed, pending, rejected |
| **Performance** | Completion rate, average turnaround time |
| **Trend** | Volume over time, and month-on-month change |
| **People** | Per-member activity, and slowest recipients |

Every number is computed from your organization's own documents. Nothing on this
page is a sample or a placeholder.

## Using it

<Steps>
### Choose a period

Use the date range control for a preset period or a custom window. Buckets switch
from daily to monthly automatically once the span passes about two months, so a
year-long view stays readable.

### Turn on auto-refresh

Pick a refresh interval if you are watching activity live — during a month-end
push, for example. The choice is remembered in your browser, so it persists
between visits without affecting anyone else.

### Read the comparison card carefully

The month-on-month figure compares **matching windows**: the first four days of
this month against the first four days of last month, not against a full month.
That is deliberate. Comparing four days against thirty produces a meaningless
number.
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  Charts use your organization's brand colour, set under
  [Settings & Branding](/organization/settings).
</Callout>

## If a figure looks wrong

**Numbers lower than expected.** The dashboard counts documents that belong to the
organization. A document created by someone before they were converted to a member
may be attributed to them personally instead.

**A completion rate of zero with documents present.** Check whether those
documents are still `PENDING` — sent but not yet signed. The dashboard counts
completions, not sends.
