# Recycle Bin

**Organization → Recycle Bin** lists deleted **Doc Manager** records, with who deleted
each one and when, so an Org Admin or DMS Admin can restore it.

<Callout type="warning">
  **This is the Doc Manager recycle bin only.** Documents deleted from **E-Sign** do not
  appear here — they follow completely different rules, described below. Do not rely on
  this page to recover a document you were sending for signature.
</Callout>

## Doc Manager records

Deleting a record moves it here and marks it `DESTROYED`. Its file and metadata are
retained, so **Restore** puts it back intact.

**Permanently delete** removes it for good. That action is restricted to **Org Admin**
and **DMS Admin** — deliberately, because it is the one irreversible step in the
records lifecycle.

<Callout type="info">
  The interface describes a 30-day retention period, and each item is stamped with an
  expiry date accordingly. **Nothing currently acts on that date** — no scheduled job
  purges expired items, so a deleted record stays here until someone permanently
  deletes it by hand. Treat the 30 days as the intent, not as an automatic cleanup you
  can count on.
</Callout>

For records under a retention schedule, dispose of them through
[Retention & Disposal](/dms/retention) rather than deleting them here. That path
records the decision and who approved it, which is what an auditor asks for.

## Deleting an E-Sign document is different

E-Sign deletion depends on the document's status, and **only one of the two cases is
recoverable at all**.

| Deleting a… | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Draft** or **Pending** document | **Deleted outright, immediately and permanently.** Not recoverable, and it does not appear in this Recycle Bin |
| **Completed** document | Hidden from your lists but retained. Not shown here either, and not restorable through this page |

<Callout type="warning">
  **Deleting a draft cannot be undone.** A document you have spent time preparing —
  recipients added, fields placed — is gone the moment you delete it. There is no
  recycle bin for it and no restore. Check twice.
</Callout>

## What deleting an E-Sign document does to signing links

**A draft or pending document is removed entirely, so its signing links stop working
immediately.** Anyone who opens a link they were sent gets an error. Deletion is
therefore an effective way to stop a document being signed.

That said, **cancel is the better tool** when a document is out for signature. Cancel
withdraws it and leaves the record — who it went to, when, and that it was withdrawn —
whereas deleting a pending document destroys that history along with the document.

A **completed** document is already signed, and re-signing is refused regardless of
deletion, because completion requires the document to be pending. Recipients do keep
whatever copy they already received by email; deleting in HubSign does not reach into
anyone's inbox.

## In short

- **Recycle Bin** = Doc Manager records. Restorable.
- **Delete a draft or pending E-Sign document** = permanent. No safety net.
- **Want to stop a document being signed but keep the record?** Cancel it.
- **Want a document kept for a defined period?** File it in
  [Doc Manager](/dms/documents), where [retention](/dms/retention) is enforced by a
  schedule with a review step.
