# Billing & Seats

**Organization → Billing** shows your subscription, seat count and current period as
provisioned for your tenant.

<Callout type="info">
  **Seats are purchased in the WorkHub console**, under *My Subscription* — not on
  this page. HubSign reflects what your tenant has been allocated.
</Callout>

## Where each thing lives

| | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| Buy or change seats | WorkHub console → *My Subscription* | Subscription and quantity |
| See seats in use | HubSign → Organization → Billing | Allocation and usage |
| Assign a seat to a person | HubSign → [Members](/organization/members) | Convert or invite |
| Mailbox licences for email intake | WorkHub console → *Email → Mailboxes* | The address vendors email |

## What consumes a seat

A seat is consumed **per member who logs in** — not per document, per signature, or
per recipient.

**Recipients never consume a seat.** People you send documents to sign from an
emailed link without an account.

Converting a pending user to a member is the moment a seat is spent, and conversion
is refused when none are free. See [Members & Seats](/organization/members).

## Running out of seats

You will notice it as a refused conversion or a refused invitation. Buy seats in the
WorkHub console; the new allocation appears here, and the conversion then succeeds.

If someone has left, remove them from Members first — that frees their seat
immediately and costs nothing. Their documents stay with the organization.
