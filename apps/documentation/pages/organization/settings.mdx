# Settings & Branding

**Organization → Settings** is a set of cards. **Each card saves independently** —
press the save button in the card you changed.

| Card | Controls |
|---|---|
| **General** | Name, slug, domain, logo |
| **Branding** | Colours applied across the app, emails and dashboard charts |
| **Signed Documents** | Whether the audit certificate is attached to signed PDFs |
| **Sign Reminders** | Chase recipients who have not signed |
| **Email-to-Sign** | The inbox address for [invoice intake](/organization/signature-inbox) |
| **Single Sign-On** | Your own identity provider |
| **Member domains** | Restrict membership to your email domains |
| **Security** | Enforce one session per user |
| **Confidentiality** | Default classification for new documents |

## Branding

Set your primary and accent colours, sidebar, and button colours. These are applied
to the app, to the emails your recipients receive, and to the
[Dashboard](/organization/dashboard) charts, so a recipient sees your brand rather
than a generic one.

## Signed Documents — the audit certificate

The **Final Audit Report** is the page appended to a signed PDF recording who
signed, when, from where, and the document's integrity hash. It is your evidence
that the signature happened.

Turn it off only if your documents are circulated externally and the extra page is
unwanted.

<Callout type="warning">
  This applies at **seal time**, so it only affects documents completed from now on.
  Documents already signed keep the pages they were sealed with — the certificate is
  baked into the PDF and cannot be removed retroactively.
</Callout>

For documents already signed, use the **Download** menu on the document page and
choose *Download without audit certificate*. That gives you the signed pages alone
while leaving the sealed original intact.

## Sign Reminders

Enable reminders, then set the interval in days and a maximum count. Recipients who
have not signed are emailed automatically until they sign or the maximum is reached.

## Single Sign-On

<Steps>
### Register HubSign with your provider

Create an application in Microsoft 365 / Azure AD, Google Workspace, Okta or Auth0
and note the client ID and secret.

### Enter the details here

Paste the well-known URL, client ID and secret, and give the button a label your
staff will recognise.

### Share the sign-in link

Members sign in at `/signin?org=<your-slug>`.
</Steps>

## Member domains and self-signup

Adding a domain such as `yourcompany.com` means people registering with that domain
appear in [Members](/organization/members) as pending, ready to convert.

Combine it with **Disable self-signup** to stop staff creating their own accounts
outside your control — they are told the organization already exists and to ask an
administrator.

<Callout>
  Domain restriction affects **membership**, not signing. External recipients can
  still be sent documents and still sign them, regardless of their email domain.
</Callout>
