# OCR Queue & AI

## OCR Processing Queue

**Repository → Processing** tracks OCR status for uploaded documents.

OCR is what makes a scan's **contents** searchable. Until it has run, a document is findable
by its metadata only — see [Search](/dms/search).

### If nothing is processing

The queue depends on the **BMS ML service** being configured for your organization. Until it
is, uploads will sit unprocessed. The page says so, and links to the settings.

Configuration lives under **Settings → Organization → Profile & Branding**, where the service URL and credentials
are set. This is the same service the
[Signature Inbox](/organization/signature-inbox) uses to read invoices.

### After a bulk import

Expect a backlog. A [bulk upload](/dms/bulk-upload) of several hundred files will take time
to work through, and search on content will be incomplete until it finishes. Check here
before concluding that an imported document is missing text.

<Callout type="warning">
  **OCR output is a machine's guess.** In real data here, extracted purchase-order numbers
  have come out as `"Box"`, `"licy"` and `"rt"`. Treat extracted values as a search aid and a
  starting point for review — never as authoritative data feeding a financial decision
  unchecked.
</Callout>

## AI

**Tools → AI Agent** lets you ask questions about your documents, generate reports and get
insights, as a conversation.

### What it is good for

- Locating something you can describe but not name — *"the lease amendment that changed the
  break clause"*
- Summarising across records — *"what did we agree with this supplier"*
- Drafting a report from what is filed

### What to check before relying on an answer

An AI answer is generated from document content, which for scanned records means it is
generated from **OCR output**. Two layers of inference sit between the paper and the answer.

For anything consequential — a figure, a date, an obligation — open the underlying document
and read it. The AI is a way to find the right document quickly, which is genuinely valuable;
it is not a substitute for reading the record you are about to act on.

<Callout type="info">
  Answers are limited to documents you are permitted to see, so two people can legitimately
  get different answers to the same question. See [Permissions](/dms/permissions).
</Callout>
