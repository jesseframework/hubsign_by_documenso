# Activity & Audit

**Home → Activity** is the audit log for the records library: who did what, to which
document, and when.

## What it records

Every consequential action leaves an entry — filing and re-filing, classification changes,
downloads, permission changes, retrieval requests and returns, retention decisions and
disposals.

Individual documents also carry their own history on the document page, which is usually the
faster route when you have a specific record in question.

## What it is actually for

Three uses, in rough order of how often they come up:

**Answering "where did this go?"** A record that is not where someone expects it was usually
re-filed, and the log says by whom. This resolves most "missing document" reports.

**Proving a destruction was authorised.** When a record has been disposed of, the question
later is not whether it is gone but whether it should have been. The retention decision and
who made it are here — see [Retention & Disposal](/dms/retention).

**Demonstrating access control.** Being able to show who downloaded a `RESTRICTED` document,
and that nobody else did, is often the actual requirement behind a confidentiality policy.

<Callout type="info">
  For signed documents, the signing audit trail is separate and lives on the document in
  E-Sign, alongside the audit certificate in the sealed PDF. The DMS log records what happened
  to the record; the certificate records what happened at signing.
</Callout>

## A suggested practice

Read it periodically rather than only when something goes wrong. Two patterns are worth
noticing early:

- **Re-filing churn** — the same records moving repeatedly usually means the
  [filing structure](/dms/filing) does not match how people think about the documents
- **Download patterns that do not match roles** — often a sign that
  [permissions](/dms/permissions) were granted ad hoc and never revisited

Both are easier to fix as habits than as incidents.

## What the log cannot tell you

It records actions in HubSign. It cannot record what happened to **paper** — whether a box was
actually shredded, or whether a retrieved file made it back to the right shelf.

That gap is why the [retrieval](/dms/retrievals) states and the disposal statuses need to be
kept honest by the people handling the physical records. The log is only as truthful as the
statuses people set.
