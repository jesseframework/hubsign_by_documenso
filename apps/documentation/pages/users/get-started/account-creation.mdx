# Getting Your Account

HubSign is a cloud service on **WorkHub Cloud**. There is nothing to install —
your organization is provisioned as a tenant, and an administrator grants you
access to it.

<Steps>
### Get access

There are two ways an account is created:

- **An administrator invites you.** You receive an email with a link to set your
  password and sign in.
- **You register with a company email address.** If your organization has
  restricted membership to its own email domains, registering with an address on
  one of those domains puts you in the members list with **pending** status. An
  administrator then converts you to a full member, which assigns you a licensed
  seat.

<Callout>
  If self-signup has been disabled for your organization, registering will tell you
  the organization already exists and to ask an administrator to add you. That is
  expected — it keeps accounts under your administrator's control.
</Callout>

### Sign in

Sign in at your organization's HubSign address in any modern browser. Nothing is
installed on your machine, and you can sign in from anywhere.

If **Single Sign-On** is configured, use the SSO link for your organization
(`/signin?org=<your-organization-slug>`) and authenticate with your normal company
identity provider — Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Okta or Auth0. You will not
need a separate HubSign password.

<Callout type="info">
  Signing does not require an account at all. People you send documents to open a
  private link from their email and sign in the browser, whether or not they are in
  your organization.
</Callout>

### Secure your account

Set up two-factor authentication or a passkey. See
[Account security](/users/get-started/account-security).

<Callout type="info">
  Your organization may enforce a single concurrent session per user. If so, signing
  in somewhere new ends your other session.
</Callout>

### Send your first document

Go to **E-Sign**, upload a PDF, add recipients and place their fields. The
[User Manual](/manual#32-preparing-a-document) walks through it step by step.

</Steps>
