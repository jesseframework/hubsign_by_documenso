# Teams vs Organization

HubSign has two ways of grouping people. Knowing which one you use saves looking for
settings in the wrong place.

| | Organization | Team |
|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | Your company as a whole — the tenant | An optional sub-group within it |
| **Holds** | Members, seats, branding, rules, workflows, the inbox, DMS | A narrower document scope with its own preferences |
| **You use** | **This one** | Not in use |

<Callout type="info">
  Your deployment is organization-based. Every document belongs to the organization, and
  the settings you are likely looking for are under **Organization**, not under a team.
</Callout>

## Where to go instead

| Looking for | Go to |
|---|---|
| Branding and colours | [Settings → Organization → Profile & Branding](/organization/settings) |
| Who can see which documents | [Settings → Organization → Profile & Branding](/organization/settings) (confidentiality), and [Access Control](/dms/permissions) |
| Adding and removing people | [Settings → Organization → Members & Roles](/organization/members) |
| Email wording and sender behaviour | [Settings → Automation → Email Templates](/organization/email-templates) |
| Everything else | [Organization Overview](/organization) |

## If you do add teams later

Teams carry their own document visibility, email sender details and branding preferences,
which override the organization's for documents in that team's scope. That is worth knowing
before creating one: a team with unset preferences does not inherit silently in every case,
and it becomes a second place to check when a setting appears not to apply.

Unless you have a clear reason to separate a group's documents from the rest of the
organization, the organization on its own is simpler to administer.
