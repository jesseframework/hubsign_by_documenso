# Support

## Start with your own administrators

Most questions are answered fastest by an **Org Admin** in your organization, because
most of what people get stuck on is configuration rather than a fault:

| Question | They control it under |
|---|---|
| I cannot sign in, or I need access | [Members & Seats](/organization/members) |
| A rule is blocking my signature | [Business Rules](/organization/business-rules) |
| My approval is stuck with someone | [Approvals](/organization/approvals) |
| Invoices are not arriving in the inbox | [Signature Inbox](/organization/signature-inbox) |
| The wrong logo or colours are showing | [Settings & Branding](/organization/settings) |
| I need a document restored | [Recycle Bin](/organization/recycle-bin) |

## Tenant-level requests

Seats, mailboxes, domains and single sign-on are provisioned in the **WorkHub
console** rather than in HubSign. An Org Admin with access there can handle:

- buying or reassigning seats
- creating the mailbox the Signature Inbox reads from
- adding a domain

## Contact support

For anything that is not configuration — something behaving incorrectly, or a
tenant-level change your administrators cannot make:

**Email:** <a href={`mailto:${SUPPORT_EMAIL}`}>{SUPPORT_EMAIL}</a>

<Callout type="info">
  Include the **document ID or reference number**, what you expected, and what
  happened instead. For a signing problem, say whether you were the sender or a
  recipient — the two follow different paths through the system and the answer
  usually differs.
</Callout>

### What helps most

- **The exact message.** A refused signature usually carries a message written by
  your own organization; quoting it identifies the rule immediately.
- **When it happened.** Every document keeps an audit log, so a time narrows it down.
- **Whether it ever worked.** A thing that stopped working points somewhere very
  different from a thing that never worked.

## Before reporting a data problem

If a figure looks wrong on an invoice, check the extracted data first. OCR is a
machine's guess, and a misread is far more common than a fault — see
[OCR Queue & AI](/dms/ocr-ai) and the review step in the
[Signature Inbox](/organization/signature-inbox).

## Known limitations

Some behaviour is a current limitation rather than a fault, and is documented as
such. Check [Known gaps](/manual#appendix-b--known-gaps) before raising it — it will
save you a round trip.
